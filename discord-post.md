**Adventurer Guide: an in-game class guide NPC for Ragnarok Offline** 🧭

Talk to the Guide in Prontera (136,171) and he reads your class and level, then tells you:

- 🗺️ **Where to level**, with a hunting tip for your class at every spot (Grimtooth crowds from Cloaking for Assassins, trap lines for Hunters, the right element for Mages, Turn Undead for Priests...)
- 🛒 **What to buy** from NPCs (prices and towns), and ⚔️ **what only drops**: slotted weapons, element katars / bows / whips, skill-bonus gear, with monster, drop rate and map
- 🃏 **Cards worth collecting** (race and element damage, Marc, Thara Frog...), slotted armor included
- 📈 **Stats and skills** with several playstyles per class (katar vs dual dagger Assassin, falcon vs trapper Hunter, Grand Cross vs tank Crusader...), rebirth and 3rd class pages
- 👹 **MVPs that might be possible** for your class, with the boss skills to look out for
- 📊 **Your server's EXP and drop rates**, read live

Pre-renewal and renewal each use their own data (maps, levels, drops, shops), all worked out from the server's own tables.

**Good to know:**
- Builds are classic community advice and the MVP ratings are only a rough pointer. A "could be possible" boss still needs good gear, potions and a Fly Wing.
- Doram and Gangsi have no page yet. Not every page is tested in game in both modes.

Download and details: https://github.com/rhewid/adventurer-guide

I'd love **feedback and wishes**: wrong levels or drops, builds you disagree with, classes you want covered more, and any bugs you hit. 🙏
